Sadhana — dedicated practice

The Power of Yoga Teacher Training

Why Become a Yoga Teacher? Training as a teacher changes your relationship with yoga itself — from something you do, to a practice you understand well enough to share.

Deepen Your Practice

Move beyond the physical postures into a fuller understanding of what you practice and why.

Build Teaching Confidence

Practice cueing, sequencing and holding space for a class in a supported training environment.

Learn Authentic Yoga

Study the tradition behind the practice — philosophy, principles and practice as one whole.

Develop Teaching Skills

Learn how to structure a class, guide a room and communicate clearly as a teacher.

Create Your Own Yoga Journey

Use your training as a foundation to shape a teaching path that's genuinely your own.
